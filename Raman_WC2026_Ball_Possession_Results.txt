FIFA World Cup 2026 — Ball Possession Analysis

Analytical question:
Does average possession among teams in the 2026 FIFA World Cup differ significantly from 50%?

Unit of analysis:
One observation per national team. Match-level possession percentages were averaged for each team.

Descriptive statistics:
n = 48
Mean = 47.27%
Median = 47.85%
Standard deviation = 8.57
Minimum = 27.80%
Q1 = 41.75%
Q3 = 54.78%
Maximum = 63.50%

95% confidence interval for the mean:
[44.78%, 49.76%]

Hypotheses:
H0: mu = 50%
H1: mu != 50%

One-sample t-test:
t(47) = -2.209
p = 0.0321

Decision at alpha = 0.05:
Reject H0

Interpretation:
The mean team possession is 47.27%. The 95% confidence interval is
44.78% to 49.76%. Since p = 0.0321 is below 0.05,
the sample provides evidence that the mean possession across teams differs
significantly from 50%. In this dataset, the observed mean is below 50%.

Data-quality note:
The raw match_team_stats.csv contains some matches where the two listed
possession percentages do not sum to 100%. Results above use the possession
values exactly as supplied in the uploaded dataset and aggregate them by team.
