FIFA World Cup 2026 — Squad Age Analysis

Analytical question:
Do higher-ranked teams have a significantly different average squad age
than lower-ranked teams?

Method:
Player ages were calculated on 11 June 2026, the tournament opening date.
Each team's mean squad age was then calculated. Teams were split at the median
pre-tournament FIFA ranking (26.5).

Higher-ranked group: n = 24, mean squad age = 27.95, SD = 1.05
Lower-ranked group:  n = 24, mean squad age = 27.93, SD = 1.20
Mean difference = 0.02 years

95% CI for mean difference:
[-0.63, 0.68] years

Hypotheses:
H0: mean squad age is equal between higher-ranked and lower-ranked teams
H1: mean squad age differs between the two groups

Welch two-sample t-test:
t(45.22) = 0.070
p = 0.9446

Decision:
Fail to reject H0

Conclusion:
There is insufficient evidence that higher-ranked and lower-ranked teams differ in average squad age.
