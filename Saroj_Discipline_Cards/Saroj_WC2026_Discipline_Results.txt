FIFA World Cup 2026 — Discipline Analysis

Analytical question:
Do teams with higher foul rates receive significantly more yellow cards per match
than teams with lower foul rates?

Method:
Teams were divided into two equal groups using the median average fouls per match
(11.125 fouls per match). Yellow-card rate was calculated as total
yellow cards divided by matches played. A Welch independent two-sample t-test was used.

Descriptive statistics:
High-foul group: n = 24, mean yellow cards/match = 0.256, SD = 0.357
Low-foul group:  n = 24, mean yellow cards/match = 0.118, SD = 0.206
Mean difference (High - Low) = 0.137

95% confidence interval for the mean difference:
[-0.033, 0.308] yellow cards per match

Hypotheses:
H0: mean yellow-card rate is equal for high-foul and low-foul teams
H1: mean yellow-card rate differs between high-foul and low-foul teams

Welch two-sample t-test:
t(36.76) = 1.629
p = 0.1118

Decision at alpha = 0.05:
Fail to reject H0

Conclusion:
The high-foul group averaged 0.256 yellow cards per match, compared with
0.118 for the low-foul group. Since p = 0.1118 is greater than 0.05,
there is insufficient evidence in this dataset to conclude that the two groups have
different mean yellow-card rates.

Data note:
The analysis uses event records labelled "Yellow Card" and "Red Card" from
match_events.csv, match counts and fouls from match_team_stats.csv, and team names
from teams.csv.
